## Permanent Plant Monitoring

Compact, permanently installed monitoring gateways featuring built-in 4G data communication for daily automatic fault detection on rooftop solar plants

• 97% connectivity rate from inside metal electrical cabinets to the cloud

• No drilling or cutting required, magnetic mounting even on steel cabinet doors

• Daily automatic time synchronization of all gateways for accurate fault timestamps

• Advanced internal sensors: tamper sensor to detect theft or removal, humidity and temperature sensors, and a charge gauge for precise backup battery life prediction
